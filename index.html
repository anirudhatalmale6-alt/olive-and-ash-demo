01 — Our story

We buy what looks best that morning, then work out dinner.

There is no fixed menu here because there is no fixed market. Whatever the growers and the boats have on the day is what the kitchen builds around, and the board is rewritten by four o'clock.

It makes for a smaller list than most places, and occasionally we run out of the thing you came for. We think that is a fair trade.

Ten ingredients, treated properly, beats forty treated carelessly.

Marta Oyelaran, Head Chef
